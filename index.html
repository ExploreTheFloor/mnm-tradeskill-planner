Tradeskill Route Planner

Monsters & Memories. Tick the professions you want to level, enter where you are now and where you want to end. The route levels them together: when a step needs parts another ticked profession makes, you make them yourself and that profession levels too.

Where your materials come from

Each choice adds to the one above it. The route only uses materials you can get this way.

Assumptions

points above your skill

The game's skill-up and failure odds aren't published. The route assumes the combines per point above for every recipe that isn't trivial yet, and keeps to recipes within the window so failures stay rare.
